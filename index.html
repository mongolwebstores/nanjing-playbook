Trading: 90-day learning path

learning only, no real money yet

This is a learning plan, not financial advice. Mainland China bans crypto exchanges and crypto trading services, so don't trade crypto while you're in China. Your student residence permit also comes with conditions. Check with the international office before you trade anything with real money.

Month 1 · Foundations

Oct
  • Work through Babypips "School of Pipsology" (free).
  • Learn candlesticks, support and resistance, trend and market structure.
  • Learn position sizing and the 1% risk rule: never risk more than 1% of the account on a single trade.
  • Read Trading in the Zone by Mark Douglas.
  • Set up a free TradingView account.

Month 2 · Choose one market

Nov
  • Compare forex/gold with stocks, and choose the one whose active hours fit your 21:30–22:15 slot. The US market opens at 21:30 Beijing time (22:30 from November).
  • Choose one simple strategy and stick with it.
  • Start paper trading and journal every trade: setup, entry, stop, target, result, emotion.

Month 3 · Proof

Dec–Jan
  • Reach 50 or more paper trades.
  • Measure win rate, average R (profit or loss in units of what you risked) and maximum drawdown.
  • During exam season, cut this to 15 minutes of review a day.

Gate before real money: at least 50 paper trades with positive results. Even then, start only with money you could lose completely, and no leverage.

Business system

Miswak Mongolia and other stores
  • Daily ops, 12:50–13:20: orders, customer messages, stock levels and delivery issues in Mongolia.
  • Daily deep work, 20:30–21:30: produce one thing each night: 2 reels, a new ad set, a product page fix, or a supplier call. Mongolia shares your UTC+8 time zone, and buyers are most active in this window.
  • Tuesday and Sunday mornings: batch a full week of reels and ad creatives so weeknights stay short.
  • Saturday 19:00: review the numbers (sales, ad spend, ROAS, best and worst reel). Turn off losing ads, put more budget behind winners, and choose next week's single focus.
  • Get help: if you don't have one yet, hire someone in Ulaanbaatar for packing, delivery and customer-service replies. Your job is content, ads and decisions.

A student visa generally doesn't allow you to work in China. Keep the business run from Mongolia, and don't take Chinese clients or local jobs without checking with the international office first.

AI: learn it, then teach it

Mon + Sun 20:00–21:00
  • Rhythm: learn on Tuesday night (30 min) and Saturday afternoon (2 h), prepare on Sunday at 17:00, then teach on Sunday and Monday at 20:00. Teaching what you learned that same week is the fastest way to remember it.
  • Why it matters for Goal 1: the class pays $200, but what you learn for it can automate your store (content, replies, ads), and that's worth far more.
  • What to learn first: the tools you already use to run the business: Claude for writing and research, Claude Code for automation, AI voiceover and video for reels. Real results from your own store make the best class examples.
  • Each lesson: one topic, a live demo, one exercise the students do during class, and one piece of homework. Keep it to 60 minutes.
  • Link it to school: use your Intro to AI class (人工智能通识导论) on Wednesday for theory and your teaching for practice.

This is paid work. In China a student needs the university's approval and a part-time work note on the residence permit before taking paid work. Ask the international office.
